Granted Support

Help for contractors, gate staff and project admins

I can’t sign in

Use Forgot password? on the sign-in screen. After 5 wrong passwords the account locks for 15 minutes.

I don’t see my project

Tap + on the Project tab and enter the project code from your project admin. An admin has to approve you before the project appears.

My delivery was rejected

Open the request: every reason is listed with what to change. The most common fixes are booking at least 48 hours ahead and choosing an activity from the current look-ahead.

The gate turned my truck away

The email you received says why and what has to happen. For a same-day emergency, call the project superintendent, who can issue an emergency pass.

Delete my account

Settings → Delete my account.

Contact

Your project admin first. App problems: [support@ajaxbuilding.com]
