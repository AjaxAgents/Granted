Granted Terms of Use

Effective [date] · Ajax Building Company

1. What Granted is

Granted lets people working on Ajax Building Company projects book deliveries and check trucks in at the gate. It is provided for use on Ajax projects only. Your subcontract or purchase order, and the jobsite’s logistics rules, still apply; Granted is the tool used to follow them.

2. Your account

  • Use your own work email and keep your password private. You are responsible for activity on your account.
  • Give accurate information in your profile and in delivery requests.
  • Project admins decide who joins their project and what access they have, and may remove access at any time.

3. Acceptable use

Do not share gate passes with anyone other than the driver of that delivery, try to get around the delivery rules or a gate check, access projects or data you have not been given access to, interfere with the service, or use it for anything unlawful.

4. Gate decisions

A green result means the delivery was approved under the project’s rules. Gate staff and the project team may still refuse entry for safety or site reasons. An approved booking does not guarantee unloading at a particular time.

5. Availability

We aim to keep Granted available but it may be interrupted for maintenance or reasons outside our control. If Granted is unavailable, follow the jobsite’s backup procedure.

6. Privacy

Our Privacy Policy explains how information is handled.

7. Changes and ending

We may update these terms and will post the new version here. You can stop using Granted and delete your account at any time in Settings.
