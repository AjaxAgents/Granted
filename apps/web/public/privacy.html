Granted Privacy Policy

Effective [date] · Ajax Building Company, [address] · [privacy@ajaxbuilding.com]

Granted is a work app for booking deliveries to Ajax Building Company jobsites and checking trucks in at the gate. This policy explains what information Granted collects, why, who can see it, and the choices you have.

What we collect

InformationWhy
Name, work email, phone number, companyTo create your account, show who booked a delivery, and contact you about your deliveries and project access.
PasswordTo sign you in. It is stored only as a salted, one-way hash (or held by Microsoft Entra if your organisation signs in that way). We cannot read it.
Project membership and access levelTo show you the right parts of each project and to let project admins manage their team.
Delivery requests (material, quantities, dates, gates, truck and carrier, onsite contact)To run the delivery rules, issue gate passes and plan the jobsite.
Gate log (pass scanned, time, result, who checked it)Jobsite safety and security, and the delivery record.
Technical logs (time, IP address, device type, errors)To keep the service secure, prevent abuse (for example, too many sign-in attempts) and fix problems.

Camera. The camera is used only when you tap Scan, to read a gate pass QR code. Images are processed on your device and are not stored or sent to us; only the code that was read is sent.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos library, health or financial information. Granted has no advertising and does not track you across other apps or websites.

Who can see your information

  • Project admins on projects you belong to or ask to join see your name, email, phone and company, and that project’s deliveries.
  • Access Control (gate staff) on a project see that day’s approved deliveries, including company, load and onsite contact.
  • Other contractors do not see your company’s deliveries.
  • Service providers that run Granted for Ajax under contract: Microsoft (Azure hosting, database, email delivery and sign-in). They process data only on our instructions.
  • We may disclose information when required by law, or to protect people’s safety on a jobsite.

We do not sell or share your personal information for advertising.

How long we keep it

Account information is kept while your account is open. Delivery records and gate logs are kept for the life of the project plus [Ajax’s records period, e.g. 7 years] because they are construction and safety records. Technical logs are kept for [90] days.

Your choices and rights

  • Update your name, phone and company in Settings → Profile.
  • Delete your account at any time in Settings → Delete my account. This removes your profile, sign-in, project access and messages. Delivery records and gate logs stay on the project as business records, with your name and contact details removed.
  • Access or correct your information, or ask a question, by emailing [privacy@ajaxbuilding.com]. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights (for example under state privacy laws); we will honour them as the law requires.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Access is limited to people who need it, every action is checked against your access level, and important actions are logged. No system is perfectly secure; tell us at the address above if you think your account has been misused.

Children

Granted is for people working on construction projects and is not intended for anyone under 18.

Changes

We will post changes here and update the effective date. If a change is significant, we will tell you in the app or by email.
